The Story Behind the Song

Magkaibang Mundo was inspired by the relationship between Otis and Ruby in Sex Education. Their contrasting personalities and different social worlds inspired the idea of two people who seem unlikely to fit together, yet discover an unexpected connection.

Sometimes, love brings together two people who see life differently. They may come from different worlds, but that doesn't mean their hearts can't find a way to meet. ❤️
