ABOUT THE ARTIST

알윈 아놀린

Independent Filipino Artist · Creator of ILTVIBE

ILTVIBE - I LIKE THE VIBE.

I started ILTVIBE just to turn my random ideas, feelings, and experiences into actual songs. Most of my tracks lean toward OPM Alternative Pop-Rock and Cyber-Anito Neo-Folk, though I like experimenting with other sounds depending on the mood.

I use AI tools to help with the production, while all the concepts, lyrics, and creative decisions come directly from me. For me, making music isn't about chasing trends or trying to be perfect. It's just about creating songs I genuinely enjoy and sharing that vibe with others.

Some of the lyrics I use today were actually written a long time ago. Back then, I didn't have a way to turn them into actual songs, and hiring a singer wasn't something I could afford. When I discovered AI music tools, I finally had a way to bring those old lyrics and ideas to life.

Creating a song also takes time. Some take around three weeks, while others take one, two, or even three months. I generate different versions, listen to them repeatedly, experiment with sounds, and keep working until I find the version that matches what I have in mind. That's a big part of how I create music under ILTVIBE.
