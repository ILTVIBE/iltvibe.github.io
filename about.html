ABOUT THE ARTIST

ALWIN ANOLIN

Independent Filipino Artist · Creator of ILTVIBE

I'm Alwin Anolin, the person behind ILTVIBE — I LIKE THE VIBE.

I started ILTVIBE as a way to turn my ideas, feelings, and experiences into music. I enjoy exploring different sounds, writing lyrics, and creating songs that each have their own story.

My music is mainly rooted in OPM Alternative Pop-Rock and Cyber-Anito Neo-Folk, but I also enjoy experimenting with different styles and sounds.

I use AI tools as part of my music-making process, but the ideas, lyrics, creative direction, and decisions behind each song come from me.

For me, music is not always about being perfect or following trends. Sometimes, it's simply about creating something that I enjoy listening to and sharing it with others.

ILTVIBE is where I bring those ideas to life.
