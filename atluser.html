The Story Behind the Song

ALTLUSER was created while I was working on a DIY smartphone projector and coding. As I experimented with technology and worked through different ideas, the song became an expression of the same mindset behind the process: keep building, keep learning, and let the results speak for themselves.

Its message is about pursuing a dream quietly, learning from mistakes, and continuing even when nobody is watching. Not every plan needs to be announced before it is finished. Sometimes, the best way to prove what you can do is to keep working until the vision becomes real.

ALTLUSER captures that spirit in four simple words: “Wala munang salita. Gawa muna.”

A creation born in the middle of another creation.
