The Story Behind the Song

ELAINE (ENIALE)

ELAINE was inspired by a girl the songwriter came across on TikTok. During her live streams, her cheerful energy, cute expressions, and constant smiles made an ordinary moment feel brighter.

Sometimes, inspiration comes from the simplest things—a smile, a laugh, or the happiness someone brings without even trying. Her lively presence became a spark for a song that carries the same lighthearted feeling.

ELAINE is about admiration and the joy of discovering someone whose smile can lift your mood. It turns that little burst of happiness into a melody, preserving the feeling long after the live stream has ended.

Some people appear on your screen for a moment, but the feeling they leave behind becomes a song.
