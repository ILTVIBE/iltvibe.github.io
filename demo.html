BEHIND EVERY ILTVIBE SONG

Making a song takes time. Some songs take me around 3 weeks, while others take 1, 2, or even 3 months before I find a version I'm happy with.

I usually make several versions, listen to them over and over, and try different sounds until I get what I'm looking for. Sometimes I think I've found the right one, then I try again because I feel something is still missing.

That's part of making music for me. I don't want to rush it. I just want each song to sound the way I imagine it.

- ILTVIBE
