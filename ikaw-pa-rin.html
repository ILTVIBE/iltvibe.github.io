The Story Behind the Song

Ikaw Pa Rin was inspired by the love story of Allie and Noah in the first half of The Notebook. Their playful arguments, emotional moments, and undeniable connection inspired the song's story of two people who may fight and say goodbye, yet somehow keep finding their way back to each other.

Because sometimes, no matter how complicated love becomes, it's still the same person your heart chooses. ❤️
