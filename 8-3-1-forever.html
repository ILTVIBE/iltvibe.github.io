The Story Behind the Song

I wrote this song about a kind of love I've always wanted, growing old with someone and making a promise at the altar. Honestly, I don't think I'll ever experience this in real life, so I turned that dream into a song instead. 8-3-1-6-15-18-5-22-5-18 is my imagined forever, a love story I chose to keep alive through music.
