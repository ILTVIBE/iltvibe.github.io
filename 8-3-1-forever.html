The Story Behind the Song

I wrote this song about a kind of love I have always wanted to experience—a love sealed with a promise, shared at the altar, and carried through the years until two people grow old together.

The lyrics imagine a day when prayers are answered, hands are held, and two hearts promise to choose each other through every hardship and every joy. The code becomes more than a sequence of numbers; it represents a promise that transcends language, distance, and time.

But behind this imagined wedding is a more personal feeling. I wrote this song because I no longer believe I will experience this kind of love in my own life. Instead of leaving that dream unwritten, I turned it into a song—a place where the promise I may never make in reality could still exist.

Perhaps I cannot change what I believe my future will be, but through music, I can give this dream a voice.

8-3-1-6-15-18-5-22-5-18 is my imagined forever—a love story written not from a life I have lived, but from a dream I chose to preserve in music.
