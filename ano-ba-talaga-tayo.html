The Story Behind the Song

"Ano Ba Talaga Tayo?" was inspired by Sofie and Angelito from A Secret in Prague. Their connection brings to mind the confusing yet sweet space between friendship and romance — the kind of relationship where the feelings seem real, the jealousy slips out, and the moments feel like something more, even without an official label.

This song captures that playful uncertainty: two people acting like a couple while leaving one question unanswered — ano ba talaga sila?

An ILTVIBE original inspired by their story.
