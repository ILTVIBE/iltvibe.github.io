The Story Behind the Song

IG OFFICIAL is the next chapter in the story of Sofie and Angelito from A Secret in Prague.

Their story began with FUBU, when their connection was real but their relationship had no clear label. There were moments of closeness, quiet affection, and feelings that neither seemed ready to define.

Then came Ano Ba Talaga Tayo?—the question that gave voice to the uncertainty between them. What were they, really? Was their connection going somewhere, or would they remain caught between friendship and something more?

IG OFFICIAL imagines what happens when that uncertainty finally ends. Sofie and Angelito no longer have to hide their affection or wonder where they stand. They can hold hands openly, introduce each other with confidence, and celebrate a relationship they no longer need to keep secret.

The title represents more than making a relationship official on social media. It symbolizes the moment when two people stop guessing, choose each other, and allow the world to know what they mean to one another.

From FUBU, to asking what they really are, to finally becoming IG OFFICIAL, this song marks a more certain and mature chapter in Sofie and Angelito's love story.

Sometimes, the happiest part of a love story is not the first spark. It is finally knowing that you both want the same thing.
