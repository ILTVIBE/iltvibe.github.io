The Story Behind the Song

IG OFFICIAL is the final part of Sofie and Angelito's story from A Secret in Prague, following FUBU and Ano Ba Talaga Tayo?. It's about finally getting past the guessing games, putting a real label on things, and being proud to show the world that they're together.
